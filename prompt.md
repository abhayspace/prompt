**Act as a college canteen business strategist and operations manager.**

I have a budget of **₹10,000** to improve my college canteen for **one week**. Create a practical, profitable, student-friendly plan that maximizes **student satisfaction, sales, and efficient use of the budget**.

Assume a medium-sized college with approximately **500 students**, with the highest demand during lunch and short breaks.

Your plan must include:

1.  **Menu:** Suggest 8–10 affordable and popular food/drink items, including vegetarian options and at least 2 healthier choices.
    
2.  **Pricing:** Give the selling price and estimated cost price for every item, ensuring prices are affordable for college students.
    
3.  **Quantities:** Estimate how many units of each item should be prepared/purchased per day and for the full 7 days.
    
4.  **₹10,000 Budget Allocation:** Clearly divide the budget between ingredients, beverages, packaging, emergency stock, and other necessary expenses.
    
5.  **Demand Management:** Explain how to handle rush hours, prevent food wastage, manage stock-outs, and adjust quantities based on daily demand.
    
6.  **Profitability:** Estimate daily sales, daily expenses, expected revenue, and approximate profit/loss for the week.
    
7.  **Special Strategy:** Suggest one daily special/limited-time offer and simple combo meals that can increase sales without significantly increasing costs.
    
8.  **Execution Plan:** Give a simple day-by-day plan for Monday–Sunday, including how to use the first 1–2 days to understand demand and modify inventory for the remaining days.
    

**Important constraints:**

*   Total initial budget must not exceed **₹10,000**.
    
*   Keep individual items mostly within **₹20–₹80**.
    
*   Prioritize high-demand, low-waste items.
    
*   Do not assume expensive equipment or major infrastructure changes.
    
*   Clearly state any assumptions you make.
    

Present the final answer in **tables wherever possible**, followed by a short explanation of why this strategy should work.
